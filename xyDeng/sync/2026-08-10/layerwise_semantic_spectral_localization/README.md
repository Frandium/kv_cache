# Layerwise Semantic Spectral Localization Handoff

## Scope

This curated package answers one meeting question: after the failure of a
universal fine-semantic spectral right-shift and fixed-band routing, what
layer-wise representation object remains worth testing?

The meeting focus is `HUMAN_CONFIRMED`. The A15_07 design remains
`draft_human_review_required`; confirming the meeting focus does not approve
the candidate Anchor, a Protocol, implementation, or execution.

## Current Claim

Local spectral rank describes where semantic variance falls inside one layer;
it does not identify a transported semantic direction. Matched taxonomy,
shared-tail, local compatibility, and matched-training evidence do not admit a
fixed covariance band as a functional Router coordinate. The remaining
candidate is a separately constructed, cross-expression conditional-fine
direction for each layer.

## Claim Boundary

The package does not establish that deep layers lack fine semantics, that
middle or tail directions are uninformative, that A15_07 directions are used
by the model, or that a layer-wise Router would improve expert utility or NLL.

## Next Action

Review the six A15_07 design points and decide whether projection of a shared
mean direction is a required control. Even a representation-level Pass would
open only an independent expert-utility admission test, not Router training.
